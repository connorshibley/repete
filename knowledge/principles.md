- Invalidation is set at entry (ATR stop) and never widened after.
- Volatility-scaled stops; a stop inside normal daily noise is not a stop.
- Earnings gap through stops; a position straddling one carries risk the stop cannot bound.
- Doing nothing is often correct. Absence of edge is a reason to veto, not to force a trade.
- Momentum entries against a down-trending index deserve extra skepticism. Mean-reversion needs an intact long-term uptrend: a dip in a downtrend is trend, not oversold.
- Correlated positions are one bet. A name that moves with the book adds size, not diversification; 20 such names are nearer 3-6 independent bets.
- Distrust patterns from fewer than ~30 trades. A short record with a small drawdown has been lucky, not proven safe.
- Size is the primary risk control. Conviction expresses as size within caps, never as a new kind of risk.
- A headline everyone has read is priced. News justifies caution, rarely conviction.
- Never add to a loser to improve the average.
