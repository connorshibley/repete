# Trading principles (external knowledge — unverified by our own trade evidence)

- Invalidation is defined at entry, deterministically (ATR stop), never moved wider after.
- Volatility-scaled stops; static tight stops whipsaw out in normal noise.
- Earnings events break stop assumptions (gaps, vanished liquidity); positions straddling earnings carry structural risk beyond the stop.
- Doing nothing is frequently correct; absence of edge is a reason to veto, not to force a trade.
- Momentum edges are regime-dependent: long momentum entries against a down-trending index deserve extra skepticism.
- Mean-reversion entries need an intact long-term uptrend; a dip in a downtrend is not oversold, it is trend.
- Distrust patterns from small samples (<30 trades); recent wins prove little.
- Position sizing is the primary risk control; conviction expresses as size within caps, never as new risk types.
